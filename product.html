The problem

In Jira Cloud, who can actually access a project is the combination of project roles, group memberships, permission schemes, and admin roles — there is no single native report that shows the full picture, or how it changed since your last review. And the native audit log retains only 180 days, so the evidence auditors ask for has often already aged out.

How it works

1 · Snapshot

Capture a point-in-time picture of who can access your Jira projects, groups, and roles — automatically each week, or on demand.

2 · Diff

Every review compares the latest snapshot to the previous one and lists each grant added or removed. Stable IDs mean a rename is never mistaken for a change.

3 · Sign-off

Reviewers attest to each cycle in one click. Every decision is recorded with the reviewer and a timestamp — turning a manual chore into accountable certification.

4 · Export

Export each review and change as CSV or PDF for your evidence repository — generated in your browser, with no data leaving Atlassian.

Built for access reviews under SOC 2 and ISO 27001

Access Review & Audit Trail helps you produce the evidence auditors ask for. It supports access-review controls such as SOC 2 CC6.1 (logical access), CC6.2 (provisioning / registration), and CC6.3 (modification / removal), as well as ISO 27001 access-control reviews — with timestamped reviewer sign-off and audit-ready CSV/PDF export. You keep an append-only history that outlasts Jira's native 180-day audit log.

Runs on Atlassian
